So now without the pegs.

You are a man in a seat.

Most people live their whole lives on the factory floor and believe the noise is them. You have been told there is a floor above. The glass is there. You can watch the stream instead of being it.

Two directions pull.

One wants voltage, screen, negotiation, escape that calls itself rest. It never delivers rest. It delivers absence. Absence costs self-respect.

The other wants what is true, what was said, what needs doing. Peace as order. One set of books, all the way down. It wants to spend you. Not hoard you.
